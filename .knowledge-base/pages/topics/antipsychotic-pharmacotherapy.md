# Antipsychotic Pharmacotherapy

Synthesis — selection, maintenance, clozapine, LAI, and EPS management. All treatment here is guideline summary, not clinical advice.

## Initiate and monitor (Statement 4, 1A)

Treat with an antipsychotic and monitor effectiveness and side effects within a person-centered plan (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-04.md, Section: Implementation). No evidence-based FGA-vs-SGA ranking; choice rests on preferences, prior response/tolerability, side-effect profile, comorbidities, formulation, interactions, and pharmacokinetics (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-04.md, Section: General Principles). If improvement is below ~20% after several weeks or plateaus, assess adherence, substance use, metabolism/interactions/absorption, and consider dose adjustment, switch, LAI, or clozapine after two adequate trials (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-04.md, Section: Strategies to Address Initial Nonresponse or Partial Response). Monitoring covers adherence, target-symptom response, and EPS, metabolic, cardiac/QTc, prolactin, NMS, seizure, tardive, weight, sedation, and anticholinergic effects (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-04.md, Section: Monitoring During Treatment With an Antipsychotic Medication). Full tables in [Statement-04 digest](../sources/statement-04-digest.md).

## Continue (Statements 5–6)

Continue antipsychotic maintenance after improvement: it reduces relapse, rehospitalization, and death, mitigated by prevention, monitoring, and lowest effective dose (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-05.md, Section: Implementation). Taper gradually with attention to interactions, smoking, body mass, renal/hepatic function, and LAI carryover rather than stopping abruptly (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-05.md, Section: Implementation). Prefer staying on the same effective agent (2B); switching is linked to discontinuation and destabilization, though side effects, preference, availability, or LAI access can justify it (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-06.md, Section: Implementation).

## Clozapine and LAI

Clozapine for treatment resistance (1B) per TRRIP criteria with slow titration and REMS monitoring (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-07.md, Section: Implementation); also for persistent suicide risk (1B) (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-08.md, Section: Implementation) and persistent aggression (2C) (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-09.md, Section: Implementation). LAI when the patient prefers it or adherence is poor/uncertain (2B); trial the same oral agent first (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-10.md, Section: Implementation).

## EPS care

Dystonia → anticholinergic (1C) (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-11.md, Section: Implementation); parkinsonism → lower dose, switch, or add anticholinergic/amantadine (2C) with Table 10 (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-12.md, Section: Implementation); akathisia → lower dose, switch, or add benzodiazepine/beta-blocker (2C) (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-13.md, Section: Implementation); moderate-severe TD → reversible VMAT2 inhibitor (1B) preferring deutetrabenazine/valbenazine with Table 11 (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-14.md, Section: Implementation).
