# Guideline Statements

Map of the APA schizophrenia recommendations ingested here (Statements 1–14 plus the 24-statement summary). Psychosocial Statements 15–24 are summarized only via the summary sheet — the corresponding full texts are not in this repo.

## Assessment (1–3, all grade 1C)

Comprehensive initial assessment, a quantitative symptom/function measure, and a documented person-centered evidence-based treatment plan (docs/medical-docs/guideline/guideline-statement-summary.md, Section: Assessment and Determination of Treatment Plan). See [assessment workflow](assessment-workflow.md) for detail.

## Pharmacotherapy core (4–7)

Treat with an antipsychotic with monitoring (1A), continue after response (1A), preferably the same agent (2B), and use clozapine for treatment resistance (1B) (docs/medical-docs/guideline/guideline-statement-summary.md, Section: Pharmacotherapy). Statement 4 carries Tables 3–9 on dosing, pharmacokinetics, receptor binding, side effects, and LAI characteristics (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-04.md, Section: Table 3–Table 9). Statement 7 follows TRRIP: ≥12 weeks of at least moderate symptoms with impairment plus ≥2 adequate trials; target typically 300–450 mg/day and, if tolerated without response, level >350 ng/mL for ≥8 weeks with REMS ANC monitoring (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-07.md, Section: Identification of Treatment-Resistant Schizophrenia / Implementation).

## Pharmacotherapy adjuncts (8–14)

Clozapine for persistent suicide risk (1B) or aggression (2C); LAI for preference or poor adherence (2B); anticholinergic for dystonia (1C); options for parkinsonism and akathisia (2C); VMAT2 inhibitor for moderate-severe tardive dyskinesia (1B) (docs/medical-docs/guideline/guideline-statement-summary.md, Section: Pharmacotherapy). Individual grades: Statement 8 clozapine in suicide risk 1B (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-08.md, Section: STATEMENT 8); Statement 9 clozapine in aggression 2C (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-09.md, Section: STATEMENT 9); Statement 10 LAI 2B with same-oral-agent trial first (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-10.md, Section: Implementation); Statement 11 anticholinergic for dystonia 1C, vote 8–1 (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-11.md, Section: Differences of Opinion); Statement 12 parkinsonism 2C with Table 10 (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-12.md, Section: TABLE 10); Statement 13 akathisia 2C (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-13.md, Section: Implementation); Statement 14 VMAT2 inhibitors 1B preferring deutetrabenazine/valbenazine with Table 11 (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-14.md, Section: TABLE 11).

## Methods

Evidence rated High(A)/Moderate(B)/Low(C) on bias, consistency, directness, and precision; statements rated 1 = recommendation vs 2 = suggestion by modified Delphi (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Guideline-Development-Process.md, Section: Rating the Strength of Supporting Research Evidence / Rating the Strength of Guideline Statements). External review drew 98 individuals and 20 organizations in May–June 2019; APA approval followed in late 2019 with no industry funding (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Guideline-Development-Process.md, Section: External Review / Funding and Approval).
