# Panss

The Positive and Negative Syndrome Scale and its role as a quantitative treatment target. Synthesis — scale facts come from the criteria sheet; usage guidance from Statement 2.

## Scale

Clinician-rated measure of schizophrenia-spectrum severity for the previous 7 days using interview plus collateral (docs/medical-docs/PANSS.md, Section: Questions). Each of 30 items is scored 1–7 where 1 = absent and 7 = extreme (docs/medical-docs/PANSS.md, Section: Scoring system). Subscales: Positive P1–P7 range 7–49, Negative N1–N7 range 7–49, General G1–G16 range 16–112, Total 30–210 with no reverse-scoring (docs/medical-docs/PANSS.md, Section: Scoring system). Approximate anchors: 58–74 mildly ill, 75–94 moderately ill, 95–115 markedly ill, ≥116 severely ill (docs/medical-docs/PANSS.md, Section: Approximate total-score anchors). Record item and subscale scores, not only the total; percentage change = (baseline − follow-up)/baseline × 100 (docs/medical-docs/PANSS.md, Section: Interpretation).

## Limits

PANSS alone cannot establish schizophrenia, distinguish all psychosis causes, or set treatment (docs/medical-docs/PANSS.md, Section: Interpretation).

## Quantitative measures (APA Statement 2, grade 1C)

Initial evaluation should include a quantitative measure to identify symptoms and functional impairments that may be treatment targets (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-02.md, Section: STATEMENT 2: Use of Quantitative Measures). Measures complement, not establish, diagnosis: they document baseline, prioritize targets, and track pharmacological and nonpharmacological response longitudinally (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-02.md, Section: Implementation). Patient-rated scales are less time-consuming and support person-centered care; anchored self-rated scales aid EHR integration (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-02.md, Section: Implementation). Benefits were judged to far outweigh harms (time burden, possible alliance effects, misinterpretation); no writing-group differences (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-02.md, Section: Balancing of Benefits and Harms).
