# Schizophrenia

DSM-5-TR-based diagnostic guideline for schizophrenia as used by X-INSIGHT registration, plus the APA initial-assessment requirements. For BN authoring scope see [reasoning pipeline](../topics/reasoning-pipeline.md).

## Diagnosis (all six required)

No lab, imaging, genetic test, or rating scale establishes schizophrenia on its own (docs/medical-docs/schizophrenia-criteria.md, Section: Diagnostic overview).

- **A. Characteristic symptoms** — ≥2 of 5 domains for a substantial part of 1 month; at least one must be delusions, hallucinations, or disorganized speech (docs/medical-docs/schizophrenia-criteria.md, Section: A. Characteristic symptoms).
- **B. Functional decline** — clear decline below prior level in work, education, relationships, or self-care, or failure to reach expected level (docs/medical-docs/schizophrenia-criteria.md, Section: B. Functional decline).
- **C. Duration** — continuous illness ≥6 months including ≥1 month active-phase unless abbreviated by treatment (docs/medical-docs/schizophrenia-criteria.md, Section: C. Duration).
- **D. Mood/schizoaffective exclusion** — longitudinal timeline; mood episodes must occupy only a minority of the course (docs/medical-docs/schizophrenia-criteria.md, Section: D. Mood-disorder and schizoaffective exclusion).
- **E. Substance/medical exclusion** — rule out substance, medication, and medical causes (docs/medical-docs/schizophrenia-criteria.md, Section: E. Substance, medication, and medical exclusion).
- **F. Developmental** — with ASD/communication disorder, require prominent delusions or hallucinations ≥1 month (docs/medical-docs/schizophrenia-criteria.md, Section: F. Autism-spectrum and childhood communication disorders).

Terms (adequate dose/response, TRS, adherence states) follow docs/medical-docs/guideline/Glossary-of-Terms.md, Section: Clinical terms and distinctions.

## Initial assessment (APA Statement 1, grade 1C)

Comprehensive initial assessment covers presenting reason, goals/preferences, psychiatric and trauma history, substance use, treatment history, physical health, psychosocial/cultural factors, MSE with cognition, and suicide/aggression risk (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-01.md, Section: STATEMENT 1: Assessment of Possible Schizophrenia). Approach is tailored to communication, insight, severity, and culture, often spanning multiple visits, supplemented by collateral within HIPAA limits (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-01.md, Section: Implementation). Table 1 defines history/examination domains; Table 2 defines baseline and follow-up physical/laboratory monitoring including BMI, CBC, chemistries, lipids, ECG/QTc, prolactin, and movement scales (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-01.md, Section: TABLE 1 / TABLE 2). Suicide death is estimated at 4–10% in schizophrenia with higher early-course risk (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-01.md, Section: Implementation).
