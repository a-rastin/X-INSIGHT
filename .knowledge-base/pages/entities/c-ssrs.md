# C Ssrs

The Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale and clozapine guidance for persistent suicide risk.

## Scale

Ideation severity is the highest-numbered level 1–5 endorsed in the assessment period; 0 = none endorsed (docs/medical-docs/CSSRS.md, Section: Scoring system). Intensity dimensions (frequency, duration, controllability, deterrents, reasons) are recorded separately and not added to the 0–5 severity level (docs/medical-docs/CSSRS.md, Section: Intensity of ideation). Behavior categories are actual, interrupted, and aborted attempts plus preparatory acts and NSSI; injury is not required for an actual attempt (docs/medical-docs/CSSRS.md, Section: Suicidal behavior). Do not combine severity, intensity, behavior, or lethality into an unvalidated composite score (docs/medical-docs/CSSRS.md, Section: Scoring system). Recent level 4–5 ideation and/or recent suicidal behavior triggers high-risk alert requiring immediate assessment and safety action (docs/medical-docs/CSSRS.md, Section: Interpretation). C-SSRS identifies ideation and behavior; it does not diagnose a disorder or determine risk by itself (docs/medical-docs/CSSRS.md, Section: Interpretation).

## Clozapine in persistent suicide risk (APA Statement 8, grade 1B)

APA recommends clozapine if suicide attempt or suicide risk remains substantial despite other treatments, even without formal treatment resistance (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-08.md, Section: Implementation). Clozapine is associated with lower self-harm, suicide attempts, and hospitalization to prevent suicide at moderate strength of evidence (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-08.md, Section: Benefits). Serious harms include severe neutropenia, myocarditis, cardiomyopathy, NMS, seizures, and ileus; ANC monitoring and slow titration reduce risk (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-08.md, Section: Harms). Benefits were judged to far outweigh harms with monitoring; unanimous vote (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-08.md, Section: Balancing of Benefits and Harms).
