# Akathisia

Medication-induced acute akathisia: diagnosis, BARS scoring, and APA treatment options.

## Features

Requires subjective plus observed motor restlessness with a medication relationship, usually within 4 weeks of initiation or escalation (docs/medical-docs/akathisia-criteria.md, Section: Diagnostic features). Objective movements without subjective restlessness are pseudoakathisia; reassess rather than diagnosing acute akathisia (docs/medical-docs/akathisia-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation).

## BARS

The Barnes Akathisia Rating Scale has 4 items: objective /3, awareness /3, distress /3, global /5; the first three sum to a 0–9 component total while the global score is the principal severity measure (docs/medical-docs/akathisia-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation). Global score ≥2 indicates akathisia: 2 mild, 3 moderate, 4 marked, 5 severe (docs/medical-docs/akathisia-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation). BARS severity does not establish medication causation; apply timing and exclusion criteria separately (docs/medical-docs/akathisia-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation). Correct BARS global range is 0–5, not 0–4 as some reproduced forms state (docs/medical-docs/akathisia-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation).

## Treatment (APA Statement 13, grade 2C)

APA suggests lowering the dose, switching agent, or adding a benzodiazepine (lorazepam/clonazepam) or beta-blocker (propranolol 30–120 mg/day) (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-13.md, Section: Implementation). Akathisia mimics psychotic agitation and drives dose escalation; rates run ~10–33% even with SGAs and it can cause dysphoria and suicidality (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-13.md, Section: Implementation). Akathisia tends not to respond to anticholinergics; mirtazapine has only preliminary support (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-13.md, Section: Implementation). Benzodiazepine harms include sedation, falls, respiratory depression, and misuse; propranolol harm centers on hypotension and CYP interactions (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-13.md, Section: Harms).
