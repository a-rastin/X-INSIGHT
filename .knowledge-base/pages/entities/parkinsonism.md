# Parkinsonism

Medication-induced parkinsonism: recognition, SAS scoring, and APA treatment options.

## Features

Bradykinesia/akinesia, rigidity, or parkinsonian tremor with a plausible medication relationship, commonly within weeks of initiation or escalation; tremor may be absent and symmetry is not required (docs/medical-docs/parkinsonism-criteria.md, Section: Diagnostic features). Consider Parkinson disease, catatonia, negative symptoms, and depressive slowing; exposure can unmask Parkinson disease (docs/medical-docs/parkinsonism-criteria.md, Section: Clinical assessment and exclusions). Fever, altered consciousness, autonomic instability plus severe generalized rigidity requires urgent NMS evaluation, not routine parkinsonism attribution (docs/medical-docs/parkinsonism-criteria.md, Section: Clinical assessment and exclusions).

## SAS

The Simpson-Angus Scale uses 10 items scored 0–4, raw total 0–40, mean 0–4; higher means more severe signs (docs/medical-docs/parkinsonism-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation). Original normal mean is up to 0.3 (raw ≥4 abnormal); later 0.65/0.7 screening thresholds are population-specific, not universal (docs/medical-docs/parkinsonism-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation). SAS defines no validated mild/moderate/severe bands; do not use local cutoffs without a stated source (docs/medical-docs/parkinsonism-criteria.md, Section: Scoring and interpretation).

## Treatment (APA Statement 12, grade 2C)

APA suggests lowering the antipsychotic dose, switching agent, or adding an anticholinergic; amantadine is a lower-cognitive-burden alternative (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-12.md, Section: Implementation). Table 10 details amantadine, benztropine, diphenhydramine, and trihexyphenidyl dosing, pharmacokinetics, CYP interactions, and renal/hepatic notes (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-12.md, Section: TABLE 10). Use the lowest effective anticholinergic dose for the shortest time; distinguish parkinsonism from TD since anticholinergics can worsen TD (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-12.md, Section: Implementation). Vote was 8–1 to suggest; dissent preferred dose reduction/switch over anticholinergic (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-12.md, Section: Differences of Opinion Among Writing Group Members).
