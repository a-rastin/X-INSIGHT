# Acute Dystonia

Medication-induced acute dystonia: recognition, urgency, and APA treatment guidance.

## Features

Sustained involuntary contractions and abnormal postures linked to a causative medication, usually within days of initiation, rapid escalation, or reduction of EPS prophylaxis (docs/medical-docs/acute-dystonia-criteria.md, Section: Diagnostic features). Typical findings include neck twisting, jaw spasm, tongue protrusion, oculogyric crisis, and limb/trunk postures; throat involvement can impair speech, swallowing, or breathing (docs/medical-docs/acute-dystonia-criteria.md, Section: Diagnostic features). Establish the timeline for antipsychotics, dopamine-blocking antiemetics, and anticholinergic changes; assess distribution and persistence (docs/medical-docs/acute-dystonia-criteria.md, Section: Clinical assessment and exclusions). Exclude catatonia, primary neurologic/medical causes, and tardive disorders (docs/medical-docs/acute-dystonia-criteria.md, Section: Clinical assessment and exclusions). Stridor or breathing difficulty suggests laryngeal dystonia and requires emergency airway assessment without waiting for a checklist (docs/medical-docs/acute-dystonia-criteria.md, Section: Urgency and documentation).

## Treatment (APA Statement 11, grade 1C)

APA recommends anticholinergic treatment for antipsychotic-associated acute dystonia: IM diphenhydramine or benztropine acutely, then a short oral course (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-11.md, Section: Implementation). Acute dystonia affects up to ~10% with FGAs and <2% with SGAs; laryngospasm is rare but life-threatening and needs emergent IV/IM treatment (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-11.md, Section: Implementation). Use the lowest effective dose for the shortest needed duration; harms include dry mouth, urinary retention, glaucoma, cognitive slowing, and delirium (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-11.md, Section: Harms). Vote was 8–1 to recommend; dissent favored dose reduction/switch (docs/medical-docs/guideline/STATEMENT-11.md, Section: Differences of Opinion Among Writing Group Members).
