# Appendix D Glossary Digest

Per-source digest of Appendix D, the glossary, the development process, and acronyms.

## Evidence tables

Tables D-1 through D-13 adapt the AHRQ review, prioritizing function, quality of life, response/remission, mortality, self-harm, symptoms, and adverse events/discontinuation (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Appendix-D.md, Section: TABLE D–1). Table D-1 compares antipsychotic pairs and formulations with N, bias, consistency, directness, precision, effect size, and SOE grades; many contrasts are insufficient or low SOE (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Appendix-D.md, Section: TABLE D–1). Tables D-2 to D-13 extend the same frame to ACT, CBT, remediation, family, case management, psychoeducation, skills, employment, and dual diagnosis (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Appendix-D.md, Section: TABLE D–2–TABLE D–13).

## Terms and methods

The glossary defines adequate dose versus adequate response (50% reduction sometimes used but requiring functional benefit), FGA/SGA agents with receptor classes, and suicidality terms, plus BN-04–BN-14 naming, state, and migration conventions noting that shared names do not authorize merging evidence across networks (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Glossary-of-Terms.md, Section: Clinical terms and distinctions / Bayesian-network terminology). Guideline methods are IOM-compliant with AHRQ systematic review, High(A)/Moderate(B)/Low(C) evidence ratings, 1/2 statement grades by modified Delphi, 98 individuals and 20 organizations commenting in mid-2019, and APA approval in late 2019 with no industry funding (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Guideline-Development-Process.md, Section: Systematic Review Methodology / Funding and Approval). The acronyms sheet expands ~70 abbreviations from ACT and AIMS through VMAT2 (docs/medical-docs/guideline/acronyms_abbreviations.md, Section: Acronyms and Abbreviations).
