# Appendix C Digest

Per-source digest of Appendix C: graded review of research evidence supporting the guideline statements.

## Coverage

Evidence synthesis grading efficacy and harms across the statements, centered on the AHRQ review plus RCTs, registries, and meta-analyses (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Appendix-C.md, Section: Review of Research Evidence). Key reported signals: Statement 8 clozapine over olanzapine on suicide attempts/hospitalization (InterSePT HR 0.76, NNT 12) at moderate SOE; Statement 9 clozapine signals on aggression at low SOE with high bias and small samples; Statement 10 LAI-vs-oral RCTs showing few differences while registries suggest mortality/hospitalization benefit at moderate SOE; Statements 11–13 resting on expert opinion with tiny or old trials and no gradable body; Statement 14 deutetrabenazine/valbenazine cutting AIMS with NNT ~7/~4 at moderate SOE (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Appendix-C.md, Section: STATEMENT 8–STATEMENT 14). Table C-1 reports the placebo-controlled antipsychotic meta-analysis; Table C-2 finds only clozapine-switch and single ginkgo/vitamin-B6 signals among other TD options, all low or very-low quality (docs/medical-docs/guideline/Appendix-C.md, Section: TABLE C–1 / Table C-2).
